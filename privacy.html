Privacy Policy – Lost Context Uploader

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Lost Context Uploader is a private tool operated solely by the owner of the YouTube channel "Lost Context" (@lostcontext.official). It is used only to upload the channel's own videos, captions, titles and descriptions to the owner's YouTube account through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

With the owner's authorization, the tool accesses the owner's own YouTube channel in order to upload videos and manage their captions and metadata. It does not access any other user's data.

How data is used and stored

Authorization tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to perform uploads to the owner's channel. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used; see the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
